FAQ

Common questions

How do I tame a mob?

Hold one of the listed taming items and use it on the mob. A failed attempt can consume the item, so multiple attempts may be required.

How do I tame an Enderman?

Use an Eye of Ender or Chorus Fruit on the Enderman.

How do I tame the Ender Dragon?

First defeat the dragon and complete “Free the End”. Then resummon it and use Dragon's Breath, an End Crystal, or the Dragon Egg on the resummoned dragon.

My Red Taming Guide does not open. What can I do?

Use this online list as a fallback and include your Minecraft version, loader, and mod version when reporting the guide issue.
